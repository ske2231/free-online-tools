Free Text to Speech Reader

Paste any text and hear it read aloud. Choose a voice, adjust speed and pitch — free, no sign-up.

How to use text to speech

Paste your text, pick a voice from the dropdown, optionally adjust the speed and pitch sliders, then click Speak. Use Pause/Resume to take a break and Stop to end playback. Reading happens with your device's built-in voices, so quality varies by browser and operating system.

What is text to speech good for?

Proofreading is the killer use case: hearing your writing read aloud catches typos, awkward phrasing, and missing words that your eyes skip over. It's also great for accessibility, multitasking (listen to articles while cooking), language learning (hear correct pronunciation), and giving tired eyes a rest during long reading sessions.

Is my text private?

Yes. This tool uses the Web Speech API built into your browser (speechSynthesis), so your text is processed locally on your device and never uploaded to a server. Note that some browsers download higher-quality voices on demand; if your browser offers no voices, try Chrome or Edge on desktop.
