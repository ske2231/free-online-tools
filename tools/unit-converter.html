Free Unit Converter

Convert length, weight, and temperature units instantly. Results update live as you type.

How to convert units

Choose a category (length, weight, or temperature), type a value, and pick your "from" and "to" units. The result updates live as you type — hit the ⇄ button to swap the direction instantly.

Supported conversions

Length: millimeters, centimeters, meters, kilometers, inches, feet, yards, and miles — handy for recipes, DIY projects, and travel. Weight: grams, kilograms, ounces, pounds, and tons — useful for cooking, fitness, and shipping. Temperature: Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin — for weather, cooking, and science homework.

Why a unit converter?

Recipes from another country, product specs in unfamiliar units, weather reports while traveling — unit mismatches come up daily. This converter uses exact conversion factors and shows results to a sensible precision, so you get trustworthy answers without ads, pop-ups, or a calculator app.
