Free Password Generator

Create strong, random passwords with your choice of length and character sets. Secure and instant.

How to create a strong password

Set your desired length, choose which character types to include, and click Generate Password. You can regenerate as many times as you like and copy the result with one click. Use a unique password for every account and store them in a reputable password manager.

What makes a password strong?

Length matters more than complexity: a 16-character password with mixed character types would take centuries to crack by brute force, while an 8-character one can fall in hours. Our generator uses your browser's cryptographically secure random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues) — the same standard used by security software — so every password is truly unpredictable. Aim for at least 16 characters for important accounts like email and banking.

Are my passwords private?

Completely. Passwords are generated locally in your browser and are never transmitted over the network, stored, or logged. Close the tab and they're gone. For maximum security, avoid reusing passwords across sites and enable two-factor authentication wherever it's offered.
