Free QR Code Generator

Create QR codes for links, text, Wi-Fi logins, and vCards. Download as a crisp PNG — free forever.

How to make a QR code

Choose a content type — plain text or URL, Wi-Fi network, or contact card — fill in the fields, and click Generate QR Code. Then download the result as a high-resolution PNG for print or digital use. It's that simple, and there's no limit on how many you create.

What can QR codes contain?

QR codes can encode URLs, plain text, Wi-Fi credentials (so guests join your network by scanning), vCard contact details, and more. Restaurants use them for menus, businesses for payment links and reviews, and events for Wi-Fi access. Our generator formats Wi-Fi and vCard payloads to the standard syntax, so they work with any phone camera.

Do these QR codes expire?

No. These are static QR codes — the data is encoded directly in the pattern, not on a tracking server. They never expire, can't be deactivated, and don't track scans. Many "free" QR generators use dynamic codes that stop working unless you pay; ours work forever because there's nothing to bill you for. The QR library also runs locally in your browser, so your content is never sent to a server.
