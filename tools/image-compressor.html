Free Image Compressor

Shrink your image file size with a quality slider. Compare before and after, then download the optimized file.

How to compress an image

Pick an image, choose an output format, drag the quality slider to your preferred balance of size and quality, then click Compress & Download. You'll see the original size, compressed size, and exactly how much you saved before downloading.

How much quality should I use?

For most web photos, a quality setting of 75–85% is the sweet spot: files shrink dramatically (often 60–80% smaller) with quality loss that's invisible to the naked eye. Below 60% you'll start to notice artifacts in detailed photos, but it's fine for thumbnails and previews. PNG output is lossless, so the slider mainly affects JPG and WebP results — choose PNG when you need pixel-perfect quality, such as screenshots or graphics with text.

Why compress images?

Large images slow down websites, eat mobile data, and hit email attachment limits. Compressing before uploading is the single easiest way to speed up a website, and search engines reward fast pages. Because this tool runs entirely in your browser, compression is instant and your images are never uploaded anywhere — a big advantage over server-based compressors that queue your files and keep copies.
