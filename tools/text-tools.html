How to use the text tools

Paste your text and watch the live counters update as you type: characters, words, lines, and sentences. Click any case button to transform the whole text instantly, or use the clean-up buttons to remove extra spaces and blank lines before copying the result.

When is each case style useful?

UPPERCASE works for headings and emphasis; lowercase for normalizing data; Title Case for headlines and book titles; Sentence case for fixing text pasted from caps-lock accidents. Developers will reach for camelCase (JavaScript variables) and snake_case (Python variables, database fields) constantly.

Why count words and characters?

Word and character counts matter everywhere: social media bios, meta descriptions (around 155 characters for SEO), essays with word limits, SMS segments, and resume bullet points. Live counting means you can edit and watch the numbers update without re-pasting into another tool. Like every tool here, all processing happens in your browser — your text is never uploaded.
